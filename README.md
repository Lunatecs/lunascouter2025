Welcome to LunaScouter, A robust app to help your team succede in scouting.
LunaScouter offers a clean and simple way to manage data, add teams, and do general scouting.
It has a sleek interface that includes both alliance colors as well as both light and dark mode.

This app was made by Lunatecs 316, a team based in Salem County in Southern-New Jersey.

Learn more at https://lunatecs.org/
